## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of Japanese wabi-sabi philosophy and warm Scandinavian minimalism (Japandi). Designed for conversational food intelligence, dietary guidance, and food safety validation, the interface rejects clinical, sterile aesthetics in favor of a grounded, editorial apothecary atmosphere.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Serene Authority:** Reassuring, calm, and scientifically disciplined without inducing dietary anxiety or medical coldness.
- **Organic Precision:** Thoughtfully unhurried and tactile. Digital interactions mirror deliberate physical actions like leafing through a bespoke botanical journal.
- **Humble Sophistication:** Uncluttered negative space, quiet text contrasts, and generous proportions allow nutrition metrics, ingredient cards, and safety advisories to breathe naturally.

### Design Movement & Influence
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Evident in graceful serif display headers paired with functional humanist geometric body copy.
- **Tonal Tactility:** Relying on gentle shifts in earthen surface color rather than aggressive borders or heavy drop shadows. Rounded contours evoke river pebbles, polished stone, and ergonomic physical utensils.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural culinary and botanical elements: sun-bleached linen, dried sage, milled oats, clay earthenware, and charcoal slate.

### Role Allocation
- **Primary (`#748770`) & Primary Tint (`#8A9A86`):** Grounded sage green. Applied to interactive focus states, primary call-to-action pills, selected segment markers, and reassuring verification states.
- **Secondary Surfaces & Structure:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FAF8F5` (Soft Natural Canvas).
  - Raised Containers / Cards: `#FDFBF9` (Warm Assistant Surface).
  - Recessed Containers / Secondary Tiles: `#F4EFEA` (Warm Cream) and `#EAE3D9` (Milled Oatmeal).
- **Tertiary / Safety Guardrail (`#D97742`):** Warm Terracotta / Amber Clay. Used strictly for food allergens, cross-contamination warnings, expiration thresholds, and critical dietary caution tags. It pairs with `#FDF4ED` as its low-contrast background pill.
- **Text & Editorial Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Content: `#2D312E` (Deep Earth Charcoal) ensures effortless readability while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
  - Secondary Content: `#565D56` (Soft Forest Charcoal) for meta-labels, sub-headers, and descriptive guidance.
  - Tertiary / Passive Details: `#828C81` (Muted Herb) for timestamps, dietary units, and disabled tokens.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.18)` (Delicate Sandline) establishes structural separation without rigid visual lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the timeless editorial authority of `Playfair Display` with the clean, legible geometry of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Handled exclusively by `Playfair Display`. It sets a contemplative pace for conversational advice, culinary summaries, and food safety reviews. It should never be tracked out tightly; rather, it breathes with organic optical spacing.
- **Body & Interactive Content:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its open apertures and subtle rounded curves maintain legibility across mobile nutritional panels, ingredient listings, and conversational streams.
- **Nutritional Metrics & Ingredient Data:** Displayed using bold weight steps in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure nutritional tables and calorie tallies align vertically.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, breathing grid grounded in wide lateral gutters and spacious section breaks. Generous white space is treated as an active ingredient, reflecting wabi-sabi principles of ma (negative space).

### Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` canvas margins and `1rem` column gutters. Components stack into organic vertical cards with continuous conversational flows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Conversational inputs and nutrition summaries shift into balanced, side-by-side or stacked asymmetrical arrangements.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width with `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters. Editorial split screens allow conversational AI input on the left (spanning 5 columns) with analytical dietary breakdowns and safety dossiers on the right (spanning 7 columns).

### Rhythm & Vertical Cadence
Spacing within cards prioritizes breathing room over compact density. Inner padding on prominent surfaces maintains a minimum of `space-md` (`1.25rem`) up to `space-lg` (`2rem`). Content sections separate by at least `space-xl` (`3rem`) to preserve an unhurried, book-like pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet tonal layering and ambient environmental shadows rather than artificial, stark drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiering
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** The grounded natural linen backdrop representing ambient room tone.
- **Level 1 (Stacked Panels & Message Bubbles):** Soft surfaces tinted in `#FDFBF9` (AI Assistant Cards) or `#F4EFEA` (User Prompts). Outlined with subtle, low-opacity strokes (`rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.18)`).
- **Level 2 (Pebble Modals, Floating Bar & Allergen Warnings):** Elevated using an earthy, ultra-diffused shadow tinted with forest tones:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(100, 110, 95, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(100, 110, 95, 0.03);`
- **Level 3 (Action Overlays & Critical Alerts):** Raised with a deeper natural drop:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -6px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.08);`

### Surface Demarcation
Avoid sharp single-pixel gray dividers. Separate sub-sections using background tonal changes (e.g., nesting `#F4EFEA` within `#FDFBF9`) or delicate, semi-transparent sandline rules (`rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.18)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic tactile geometries reminiscent of river pebbles, smooth pottery, and botanic curves. With roundedness level `3`, corners are generous, soft, and fluid.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Tag Chips):** Full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provides an inviting, ergonomic feel.
- **Primary Content Cards & Containers:** Standardized on `24px` to `32px` (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) radii, yielding pebble-like frames for nutrient breakdowns and safety notes.
- **Micro Elements (Checkboxes, Toggles, Tooltips):** Scaled down to `8px` - `12px` roundedness to maintain shape harmony while preserving structural precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full-pill shape, `#748770` background, `#FAF8F5` text, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. On hover, transitions subtly to `#63745F` with an organic scale effect (`transform: scale(1.01)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Full-pill shape, background `#F4EFEA` or transparent with a delicate border (`1.5px solid rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.35)`), text `#2D312E`.
- **Destructive / Caution Button:** Warm Terracotta `#D97742` background with `#FAF8F5` text.

### Chips & Dietary Filter Tags
- **Base Tag:** Full-pill shape, text `label-md`, padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`.
- **Default State:** Oatmeal background (`#EAE3D9`), text `#565D56`.
- **Active State:** Muted Sage (`#748770`), text `#FAF8F5`.
- **Allergen Alert Tag:** Soft Terracotta tint (`#FDF4ED`), text `#D97742`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 66, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Assistant Conversational Bubbles
- **AI Response Bubble:** Surface `#FDFBF9`, border `1px solid rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.18)`, `rounded-2xl` (`1.75rem`), padding `space-md` to `space-lg`. Display headers within use `Playfair Display`.
- **User Prompt Bubble:** Surface `#F4EFEA`, borderless, `rounded-2xl`, align right, text `#2D312E`.
- **Food Safety Alert Card:** Surface `#FDF4ED`, accent border left `4px solid #D97742`, `rounded-xl`, padding `space-md`. Employs `label-lg` in terracotta for hazard identification.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Chat & Dietary Search Input:** Extended pill shape (`9999px`) or `rounded-2xl` container. Background `#FAF8F5` set against a `#F4EFEA` parent surface. Border `1.5px solid rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.24)`. Focus state reveals a soft outer ring in `rgba(116, 135, 112, 0.25)` without sharp geometric outlines. Placeholder text in `#828C81`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Selection Controls:** `20px` organic squares (`rounded-md`, `6px`) or circles. In unselected state, filled with `#FAF8F5` and outlined with `#8A9A86`. When active, filled with `#748770` bearing a clean `#FAF8F5` check icon.

### Nutrition Data Panels & Micronutrient Gauges
- **Nutrient Tiles:** Compact pebble containers (`rounded-xl`) using surface `#F4EFEA`. Number values set in `data-metric` with units set in `label-md` (`#828C81`).
- **Progress Trackers:** Soft, wide pill tracks (`8px` height) with `#EAE3D9` base and a smooth `#748770` fill bar with rounded ends.
