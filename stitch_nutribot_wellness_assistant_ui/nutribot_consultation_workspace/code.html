Critical Food Safety Recommendation

Discard Immediately • Do Not Consume

Boiling will destroy vegetative bacteria, but Bacillus cereus synthesizes an extremely heat-tolerant emetic dodecadepsipeptide toxin known as cereulide during prolonged room-temperature germination. Reheating does not render this batch safe.

Pathogen Ecology

The Spore Germination Loop

Raw rice naturally harbors dormant endospores resistant to boiling water. When cooled slowly between 50°F and 130°F (10°C–55°C), vegetative proliferation begins in under 120 minutes.

Standard Future Cooling & Storage Protocol

HACCP Domestic Protocol
1 Shallow Division

Transfer hot rice immediately into shallow glass or ceramic pans (<2 inches deep) to accelerate latent heat escape.

2 Rapid Refrigeration

Chill within 60 minutes. Keep cold storage reliably below 41°F (5°C) to inhibit spore germination completely.

3 Single Reheat

Reheat once to 165°F (74°C) internal core temperature within 3 to 4 days maximum. Never reheat twice.
